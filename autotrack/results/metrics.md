# AutoTrack - results in simulation

All numbers below are **in simulation**: the organisers' baggage replay (718 bags, 1 hour, 1 tick = 1 s) with simulated BLE radio noise, RFID reads and loader portals. The hall is a **simplified layout (28 zones, about 88 m x 66 m), not the real YYZ baggage hall**. Main run: radio seed 1. Robustness: radio seeds 1, 2, 3.

Bag-seconds = every second of every bag while it is moving or waiting in a lane (56,070 in total). The 2 scenario bags whose tag fell off or went silent are left out of the zone numbers. Zone switches are counted on the 710 normal (non-scenario) bags.

Calibrate this laptop

Every laptop hears the ESP differently, so each one needs its own numbers. Put the ESP at a measured distance from this laptop (tape measure, same height as the laptop, nobody in between), keep it still, and press Measure. Do it at 2–3 distances, e.g. 0.3 m, 1 m and 3 m. Measuring the same distance again replaces the old reading.
